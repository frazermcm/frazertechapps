VibeWardrobe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

VibeWardrobe ("the app", "we") helps you catalogue your clothing, build outfits, and get AI-powered styling suggestions. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: we use your data to run the features you ask for, we do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and you can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

VibeWardrobe is an independently published app. For the purposes of UK and EU data protection law, the app's publisher is the data controller for the information described below. You can reach us at frazertechapps@gmail.com for any privacy question, request, or complaint.

An account is required

VibeWardrobe requires you to create an account with an email address and password before you can use it. This is what allows your wardrobe to sync across devices and to be restored if you change phone. There is no anonymous or guest mode.

Information we collect

DataWhenWhere it goes
Email address and password When you create an account Stored with our authentication provider (Supabase). Your password is stored only as a salted hash — we never see it.
Photos of clothing items When you add an item to your closet Stored on your device and uploaded to our cloud storage (Supabase) so your wardrobe syncs across devices. Sent to our AI processing provider (Rork toolkit API) to remove the background and identify the item.
Photos of you Only when you use Virtual Try-On Sent to our AI processing provider to generate the try-on image. Not stored on our servers; the generated result is saved on your device, and to your photo library only if you tap Save.
Wardrobe details (item names, categories, colours, outfits, wear history) As you use the app Stored on your device and synced to Supabase. Item names and categories — not photos — are sent to the AI provider when you request outfit or packing suggestions.
Approximate location Only if you use a weather-aware feature and grant permission Your coordinates are rounded to roughly 110 metres and sent to Open-Meteo, a weather service, to fetch current conditions. The app requests coarse location only, never precise or background location. Coordinates are never stored or sent anywhere else.
Purchase status If you subscribe to VibeWardrobe Pro Handled by RevenueCat and Google Play. We receive only whether your subscription is active. We never see your payment details.

Why we are allowed to process it

Under UK and EU data protection law we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — for your account, your wardrobe data, cloud sync, and subscription status. Without these the app cannot function.
  • Consent — for camera, photo library, and location access. Each is requested at the point of use, is optional, and can be withdrawn at any time in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests — for keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect analytics or crash reporting that identifies you personally. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Service providers

  • Supabase — account authentication, wardrobe database, and image storage. Our database is hosted in the United Kingdom (London region).
  • Rork toolkit API — AI image processing (background removal, item identification, virtual try-on) and outfit suggestion generation.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Open-Meteo — weather lookup, only when you request it.

Each provider receives only what it needs to deliver its function, and none of them is permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

International transfers

Your account and wardrobe data are stored in the United Kingdom. Some of our service providers process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UK or EEA, it is protected by Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent approved safeguard under the provider's own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and wardrobe data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed from our servers immediately, as described below. Data held on your device is removed when you delete items, use "Clear all data" in Settings, or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account and data

To delete your account and everything associated with it, open Settings in the app, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, your wardrobe items and their photos, and your saved outfits from our servers. It cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app installed, email frazertechapps@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel that separately under Play Store → Menu → Payments and subscriptions.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EEA you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased — the Delete account button above does this immediately;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • object to or ask us to restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, such as camera or location access, without affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise any of these, email frazertechapps@gmail.com. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Wardrobe data is protected by row-level security rules so that one account cannot read another's data. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store payment details and never handle your password in readable form.

Children

VibeWardrobe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or to your local supervisory authority if you are in the EEA.
