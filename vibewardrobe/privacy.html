Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

VibeWardrobe helps you catalogue your clothing, build outfits, and get AI-powered styling suggestions. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version: your wardrobe lives on your device unless you create an account, photos are sent to an AI service only to power the features you actively use, you can delete everything from inside the app, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we are

VibeWardrobe is published by Frazer Tech Apps ("we", "us"). For the purposes of UK data protection law, Frazer Tech Apps is the data controller for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at fam40@msn.com.

Information we handle

DataWhenWhere it goes
Photos of clothing items When you add an item to your closet Stored on your device. Sent to our AI processing provider (Rork toolkit API) to remove the background and identify the item. If you are signed in, also uploaded to our cloud storage (Supabase) so your wardrobe syncs across devices.
Photos of you Only when you use Virtual Try-On Sent to our AI processing provider to generate the try-on image. Not stored on our servers; the generated result is saved on your device, and to your photo library only if you tap Save.
Wardrobe details — item names, categories, colours, outfits, wear history, packing lists As you use the app Stored on your device. Synced to Supabase if you are signed in. Item names and categories (not photos) are sent to the AI provider when you request outfit suggestions or a packing list.
Styling feedback — the thumbs up/down you give AI suggestions Only when you rate a suggestion Stored on your device only (the most recent ratings). Included in later AI requests so suggestions adapt to your taste.
Account details — email address, display name Only if you create an account Stored with our authentication provider (Supabase). Used solely to operate your account and sync your wardrobe.
Approximate location Only if you tap "Use my weather" in the AI Stylist and grant permission Your coordinates, truncated to roughly 100 metres of precision, are sent to Open-Meteo, a weather service, to fetch current conditions. Location is never stored by us or sent anywhere else.
Purchase status If you subscribe to Pro Handled by RevenueCat and the App Store / Google Play. We never see your payment details.
App settings and diagnostics As you use the app Settings such as your hemisphere stay on your device. The app keeps a short diagnostic log in memory that you can view under Settings; it is never transmitted anywhere unless you choose to export and send it to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect analytics that identify you personally.
  • We do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. Outfit suggestions are styling assistance, nothing more.

Using the app without an account

You can use VibeWardrobe without signing in. In that case your wardrobe, outfits, and photos stay on your device only. AI features still send the relevant image or item names to the AI provider for processing when you use them.

Why we are allowed to handle this data

Under UK data protection law we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — operating the app, your account, wardrobe sync, and your subscription. Without this data the features cannot work.
  • Consent — access to your camera, photo library, and location. You grant these through your device's permission prompts and can withdraw them at any time in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the app secure and diagnosing faults, balanced against your privacy by keeping diagnostic data on your device.

Service providers

  • Supabase — account authentication, wardrobe database, and image storage for signed-in users.
  • Rork toolkit API — AI image processing (background removal, item identification, virtual try-on) and outfit and packing suggestion generation.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Open-Meteo — weather lookup, only when you request it.

Each provider receives only what is needed to deliver its function, and none of them is permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Where your data is processed

Some of these providers process data on servers outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, we rely on the data-processing terms each provider publishes, which include the transfer safeguards required by UK data protection law. Data kept on your device stays on your device.

How long we keep it

  • On your device — until you delete the items, use "Clear all data" in Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • In our cloud storage — for as long as your account exists. "Clear all data" while signed in also removes your synced wardrobe.
  • Images sent for AI processing — sent for the purpose of that one request and not retained by us.
  • Purchase records — retained by RevenueCat and the app stores under their own policies, so that restoring purchases keeps working.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, every image you have uploaded to our storage, and all synced wardrobe items and outfits. It cannot be undone, and it does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play — cancel that in your store account settings.

If you cannot access the app, email us at fam40@msn.com and we will delete the account for you.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access);
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete (rectification);
  • delete your data (erasure) — you can do this yourself in Settings;
  • restrict or object to how we use your data;
  • provide your data in a portable, machine-readable form;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave, such as location access.

Email fam40@msn.com to exercise any of these. We will respond within one month. Exercising your rights is free and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Security

Your synced data is protected by your account credentials and stored with per-user access rules, so one account cannot read another's wardrobe. Traffic between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

VibeWardrobe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy we will update this page and note the new date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.
