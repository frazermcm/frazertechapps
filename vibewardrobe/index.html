Deleting your account and data

You can delete everything from inside the app at Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, all uploaded images, and every synced wardrobe item and outfit. To clear the wardrobe on a device without deleting your account, use Settings → Clear all data.

If you cannot access the app, email fam40@msn.com from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription — cancel that in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
