Deleting your account

You can delete your VibeWardrobe account and all associated data at any time from inside the app: open Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, your wardrobe items and their photos, and your saved outfits from our servers. It cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app installed, email frazertechapps@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel that separately under Play Store → Menu → Payments and subscriptions.
